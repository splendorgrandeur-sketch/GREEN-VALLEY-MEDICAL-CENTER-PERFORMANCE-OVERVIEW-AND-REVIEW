# 🌿GREEN-VALLEY-MEDICAL-CENTER-PERFORMANCE-OVERVIEW-AND-REVIEW
#  PERFORMANCE GAPS AND OPERATIONAL IMPACT

This is a Performance Analysis for Green Valley Medical Center, focusing on patient outcomes, treatment costs, departmental efficiency, and demographic trends. Beyond the dashboard visuals, the analysis revealed several operational challenges that directly impact performance and resource utilization.

## ⚠️ Key Challenges  
- Heavy reliance on low‑cost treatments limiting revenue growth  
- Departments with high costs but lower recovery outcomes  
- Average hospital stay of 5 days increasing operational expenses  
- Elderly and middle‑aged patients dominate admissions  
- Uneven physician performance across departments  

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations  
- Strengthen Cardiology performance through targeted quality improvement  
- Enhance elderly care pathways and preventive care programmes  
- Improve treatment effectiveness via protocol reviews and staff training  
- Introduce monthly/quarterly performance tracking  
- Integrate patient satisfaction and readmission data  
- Optimise discharge planning to reduce unnecessary hospital stays  
- Explore higher‑value specialist services to diversify revenue streams
